Campus Life Planner

1.Overview

Campus Life Planner is a web application that helps university students to organise their acade3mic tasks, manage their time and balance ther academic lives with their personal livesin one.

2.Problem

Students mostly struggle with missing deadlines, poor time management, some disorganized schedules and to balance their academics with extracurricular activities. And this planner provides a centralized platform to manage these challenges.

3.The main features

-Task management, that creates tasks, set deadlines, assign priorities, add tags, estimate duratons and track completion status.
-Dashboard, where everything is displayed; the pending, completed and over tasks and upcomming events.
-Events scheduling, for organizing lectures, club meetings, workshops and social events.
-Searc and sorting, to fint tasks and events by keywords, categories, priority or status.
-Categories and tags, to organize activities into academics, personal goals, clubs, sports and social events.
-Time management, estimating task durations and monitor daily workload.

4.How it works

Students add tasks and events, organize them by deadline and priority, search or filter activities and mark taks as completed. The dashboard updates to reflect their progress.
